🌸 Uterus (Myometrium)

Muscular Organ

The uterus is a hollow, pear-shaped muscular organ. Its thick muscular middle wall (myometrium) contracts during menstruation to help expel the endometrium lining, which causes mild period cramping.

  • ✦ Cycle Role: Expands with estrogen & progesterone; contracts under prostaglandins.
  • ✦ Health Fact: Gentle heat therapy (heating pads) relaxes uterine muscles and reduces pain naturally.

Each month, hormones stimulate the uterine lining (endometrium) to thicken in preparation for potential pregnancy. When pregnancy does not occur, estrogen and progesterone drop, and the uterus sheds this lining through the vagina. Menstrual blood is a natural mixture of blood, tissue cells, and mucus, usually lasting 2–7 days.

A cycle is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next. 21 to 35 days is typical for adults. 28 days is simply a mathematical average, not a rigid rule. Variability is especially common for teenagers.

1. Menstrual: Lining sheds (bleeding).
2. Follicular: Estrogen rises and matures an egg.
3. Ovulation: Mature egg is released (~Day 14).
4. Luteal: Progesterone prepares the body; hormone levels drop if unfertilized.

Mild uterine cramping is common. But debilitating pain that disrupts school, work, or daily life is not normal and should be checked by a doctor to rule out conditions like endometriosis or fibroids.

Sanitary pads, tampons, silicone menstrual cups, discs, and reusable period underwear are all proven safe and effective. Choice depends on comfort, access, and personal preference.
